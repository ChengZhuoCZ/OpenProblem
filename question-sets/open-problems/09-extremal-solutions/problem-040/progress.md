## Verified Boundary

- Brezis records full boundedness for \(N\le9\) and an explicit singular extremal solution on the ball for \(N\ge10\) with \(f(u)=e^u\).
- These facts establish the sharp dimensional threshold in general, but not the asserted unboundedness for every smooth bounded convex domain.
- A targeted 2023--2026 primary-source search did not locate a proof or counterexample deciding the exact convex-domain question. This is a dated literature record, not an exhaustiveness claim.
