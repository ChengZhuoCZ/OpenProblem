# Progress - problem-041

## Current Status

- Status: scoped
- Summary: Brezis Open Problem 6.2 remains open; the convex-domain singleton question is not settled by current dimension bounds.
- Last updated: 2026-08-09

## Literature Check

- Figalli--Franceschini give a general \(\varepsilon\)-regularity and singular-set framework for stable semilinear equations. For \(f(t)=e^t\), their framework gives \(\dim_{\mathcal H}\Sigma\le N-10\).
- Important provenance correction: the 2026 paper explicitly notes that the \(e^u\) dimension bound was already obtained in Kelei Wang's 2012 work; the 2026 contribution is a broader framework and additional regularity theory, not a new solution of Brezis 6.2.
- Neither result implies that a convex-domain blow-up set has exactly one point. A targeted 2023--2026 primary-source search found no theorem closing that remaining question.

## Next Actions

- Track results that control the number or topology of singularities, rather than dimension alone.
- Distinguish the general stable-solution theory from results proved specifically for extremal solutions on convex domains.
