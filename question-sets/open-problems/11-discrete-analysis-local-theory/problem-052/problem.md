# Problem 052 - Hatami--Kalai Tail-Space Strengthening of KKL

Let \(f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}\) be Boolean and let \(I_i(f)\) be its \(i\)-th influence. Suppose \(k=k(n)\to\infty\). Does there exist \(\omega(k)\to\infty\) such that, whenever

\[
\mathbb EfW_S=0\qquad\text{for all }S\subseteq[n]\text{ with }|S|\le k,
\]

one has

\[
\max_{1\le i\le n}I_i(f)\ge \frac{\log n}{n}\,\omega(k)?
\]
