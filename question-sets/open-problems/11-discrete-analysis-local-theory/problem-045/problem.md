# Problem 045 - Banach-Valued Lower Riesz Estimates Below the Endpoint

Let \(X\) be a Banach space of finite cotype, \(0<\gamma<1/2\), and \(1\le p<\infty\). Does

\[
\|\Delta^{1/2-\gamma}f\|_{L^p(X)}
\lesssim
\left(\mathbb E_\delta\left\|\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_iD_if\right\|_{L^p(X)}^p\right)^{1/p}
\]

hold with constants independent of the cube dimension \(n\)?
