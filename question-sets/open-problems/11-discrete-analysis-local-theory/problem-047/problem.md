# Problem 047 - Quantitative Euclidean Sections of Finite-Dimensional \(\ell_p\) Spaces

For finite-dimensional Banach spaces \(E,F\), let \(d(E,F)\) be their Banach--Mazur distance. If \(X\) is \(N\)-dimensional and \(1\le n\le N\), set

\[
d(X,\ell_2^n)=\min\{d(E,\ell_2^n):E\subseteq X,\ \dim E=n\}.
\]

Determine, preferably sharply in \(N,n,p\), the behavior of

\[
d(\ell_p^N,\ell_2^n).
\]
