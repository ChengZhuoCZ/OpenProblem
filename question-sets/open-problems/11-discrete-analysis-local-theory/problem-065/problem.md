# Problem 065 - The Ultimate Banach-Valued Lower Riesz Estimate

Let \(X\) be a Banach space of finite cotype and \(1<p<\infty\). Does

\[
\|\Delta^{1/2}f\|_{L^p(X)}
\lesssim
\left(\mathbb E_\delta\left\|\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_iD_if\right\|_{L^p(X)}^p\right)^{1/p}
\]

hold with constants independent of \(n\)?
