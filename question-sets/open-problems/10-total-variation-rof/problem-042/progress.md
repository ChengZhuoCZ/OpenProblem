## Verified Boundary

- Brezis records \(\nabla U\in L^\infty(\Omega)\) as the available regularity and explicitly distinguishes this from \(\nabla U\in BV(\Omega)\).
- The one-dimensional case is positive; its proof uses a transformation to an obstacle problem that the source says is restricted to one dimension.
- A targeted 2024--2026 primary-source search did not locate a paper resolving the exact \(N\ge2\) statement. This is a dated literature record, not an exhaustiveness claim.

## Next Actions
