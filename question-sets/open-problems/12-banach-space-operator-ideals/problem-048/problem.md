# Problem 048 - Duality of Entropy-Number Ideals

For \(T:X\to Y\), let \(e_n(T)\) be its \(n\)-th entropy number, and for \(0<p<\infty\) define

\[
\mathcal L_p^{\mathrm{ent}}=\left\{T:\sum_{n=1}^\infty e_n(T)^p<\infty\right\}.
\]

For arbitrary Banach spaces \(X,Y\), is

\[
T\in\mathcal L_p^{\mathrm{ent}}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
T^*\in\mathcal L_p^{\mathrm{ent}}?
\]
