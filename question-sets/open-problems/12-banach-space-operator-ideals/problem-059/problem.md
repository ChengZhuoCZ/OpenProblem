# Problem 059 - Compact Endomorphisms and the Approximation Property

Let \(\mathcal F(X)\) be the finite-rank operators on \(X\), \(\mathcal A(X)=\overline{\mathcal F(X)}\) their norm closure, and \(\mathcal K(X)\) the compact operators. If

\[
\mathcal K(X)=\mathcal A(X),
\]

must \(X\) have the approximation property?
