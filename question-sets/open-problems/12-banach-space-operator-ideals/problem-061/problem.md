# Problem 061 - The Radical Ideal and Largest Proper Operator Ideals

Let \(\mathrm{Rad}\) consist of those \(T:X\to Y\) for which \(AT\) is a Riesz operator on \(X\) for every \(A:Y\to X\) (equivalently, \(TA\) is Riesz on \(Y\) for every such \(A\)). Is \(\mathrm{Rad}\) the largest proper operator ideal?
