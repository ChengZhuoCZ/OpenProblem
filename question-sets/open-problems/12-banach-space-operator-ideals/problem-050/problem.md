# Problem 050 - Products of \(p\)-Nuclear Operators

For \(0<p\le1\), let \(\mathcal N_p\) be the ideal of \(p\)-nuclear operators. For which exponents \(0<p,q,r\le1\) does

\[
\mathcal N_p\circ\mathcal N_q\subseteq\mathcal N_r
\]

hold, where \(\mathcal A\circ\mathcal B\) denotes factorization first through \(\mathcal B\), then through \(\mathcal A\)?
