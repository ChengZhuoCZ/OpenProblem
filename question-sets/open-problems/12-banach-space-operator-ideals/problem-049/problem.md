# Problem 049 - Comparing Weyl, Hilbert, and Approximation Ideals

Let \(a_n(T),x_n(T),h_n(T)\) be the approximation, Weyl, and Hilbert numbers of an operator \(T\). With the corresponding summability ideals \(\mathcal L_p^{\mathrm{app}}\), \(\mathcal L_p^{\mathrm{weyl}}\), and \(\mathcal L_p^{\mathrm{hilb}}\), is it true that

\[
\mathcal L_q^{\mathrm{weyl}}\subseteq\mathcal L_r^{\mathrm{app}}
\quad\text{when}\quad
\frac1r=\frac1q-\frac12,\ 0<q<2,
\]

and that

\[
\mathcal L_p^{\mathrm{hilb}}\subseteq\mathcal L_r^{\mathrm{app}}
\quad\text{when}\quad
\frac1r=\frac1p-1,\ 0<p<1?
\]
