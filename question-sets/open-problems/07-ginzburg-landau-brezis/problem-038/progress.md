## Literature Check — 2026-08-09

- Chen--Wei--Yan--Yang prove finite potential energy for every entire solution with \(|u(x)|\to1\), explicitly resolving Brezis Open Problem 2.5. Combined with the finite-potential-energy classification quoted by Brezis, this closes the \(q=0,\pm1\) cases of Open Problem 2.4.
- The remaining target is the global classification for \(|q|\ge2\). Del Pino--Liu--Musso--Wei--Yang prove nondegeneracy and compute Morse indices for the standard degree-two and degree-three vortices; this is spectral information, not a classification theorem for all entire solutions.
- The 2026 paper on complex sine-Gordon is an analogue, not a result for the Ginzburg--Landau equation, so it is not treated as direct evidence for or against this problem.

Primary sources:

- H. Brezis, [*Some of my favorite open problems*](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~brezis/PUBlications/234.pdf), Open Problems 2.4--2.5.
